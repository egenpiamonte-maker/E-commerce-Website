OUR STORY

Food that fits your budget.

The Krusty Krab is a food business created for teenagers, young adults, students, senior citizens, and PWD customers who want delicious food at an affordable price.

Our main products include burgers, crispy fries, refreshing drinks, and combo meals that bring everything together in one satisfying meal.

We believe that good food should be enjoyable, affordable, and accessible to everyone.
